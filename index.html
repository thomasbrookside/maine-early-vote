Maine absentee vote: Republican share vs. 2020, same day

Towns colored by change in Republican share of Republican + Democratic accepted absentee ballots (mail + in-person absentee), 2026 so far vs. 2020 on the same number of days before Election Day (both Nov 3). Each voter is counted once, on the date their accepted ballot was received. Unenrolled, Green and Libertarian voters are excluded from the share. Towns with fewer than 25 Republican + Democratic ballots in either year are gray. 2020 absentee voting was unusually heavy (pandemic), so differences reflect who is voting early as well as how many; early shifts can fade as voting continues.
